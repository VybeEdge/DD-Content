Privacy Policy

Widgets Hub - Material You (my.dd.widgets) · Droid Decor · Last updated 18 November 2026

Widgets Hub - Material You ("the app") is a collection of home-screen widgets and wallpapers made by Droid Decor. This policy explains what information the app uses, why, and your choices. We do not have user accounts, and we do not collect, sell or store your personal information on our own servers.

1. Information that stays on your phone

Widgets can show live information. The app only uses each kind after you allow it, only to draw your widgets, and keeps it on your device. It is never sent to us.

Permission / dataUsed for
Location (approximate or precise)Local weather, sunrise and sunset. Only the coordinates are sent to the weather service (see section 2).
Calendar (read only)Showing your upcoming events on calendar widgets.
Physical activity / Health Connect (steps, read only)Showing your step count and distance on fitness widgets. Health data is read on the device, is not shared with anyone, and is not used for advertising.
Notification accessShowing the song that is playing and media controls, and notification counts, on music and notification widgets. Notification content is not stored or shared.
Usage accessShowing screen-time or recently used apps on widgets that offer it.
Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, battery, storage, alarmsShowing device status and the toggles on system widgets.
Installed apps and shortcutsLetting you choose an app, app activity or app shortcut to open when you tap a widget.
NotificationsTelling you about new widgets, wallpapers and app updates. You can turn these off in the app or in Android settings.

You can deny or withdraw any permission at any time in Android Settings › Apps › Widgets Hub - Material You › Permissions. Widgets that need it will show a placeholder instead.

2. Services the app connects to

  • Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com) – weather forecasts. The app sends only your approximate coordinates; no identifier is sent. See the Open-Meteo terms.
  • GitHub (github.com / githubusercontent.com) – downloading new widgets, wallpapers and announcements. Like any web server, GitHub receives your IP address. See the GitHub privacy statement.
  • Google Play Billing – if you buy Premium, the payment is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your card or payment details. We only receive confirmation that the purchase is valid.
  • Google AdMob – see section 3.
  • Android geocoder – turns your coordinates into a city name for weather widgets, using your phone's built-in service.

3. Advertising

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob: native ads in the lists and optional rewarded ads that unlock a widget. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, and information about the ads you see and tap, to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. This can include personalised ads where the law allows it and you consent.

  • In the EEA, UK and Switzerland the app asks for your consent with Google's consent form (UMP) before ads are personalised. You can change your choice at any time from the app's Settings › Privacy options.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings › Privacy › Ads.
  • Premium removes ads.

Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

4. Information stored in the app

Your widget settings (colours, theme, glass level, tap actions), favourites, downloaded content, daily unlock count and Premium status are saved in the app's private storage on your phone. Android may include these settings in your own Google backup. Uninstalling the app removes them.

5. Crash reports

The app does not include its own analytics or crash-reporting service. If the app crashes and you have opted in to sharing with developers in Android, Google Play may send us anonymous crash reports.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

7. Data deletion

We hold no personal data about you on our servers, so there is nothing to delete on our side. To remove everything the app stores, clear its data or uninstall it. For data held by Google (ads, purchases), use your Google account settings.

8. Security

All network connections use HTTPS. Data read for widgets stays inside the app's private storage.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top. Important changes are also announced in the app.
